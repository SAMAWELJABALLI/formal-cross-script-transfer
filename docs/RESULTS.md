# Results: where every reported number comes from

All numbers below are read from the archived files named in each row. Nothing here was recomputed with new
models. Macro-F1 is ×100. Δ is a difference in macro-F1 points, with its 95% bootstrap CI.

## Pilot and H-C (dev only, pre-registration)

`reports/pilot/pilot_analysis.md` and `reports/hc/hc_analysis.md` hold the dev-only pilot and the H-C analysis that
fixed the protocol. `reports/pilot/decision_*.md` are the raw outputs of `formal_si.decide`. Their printed "GO"
is computed from oracle-selected point estimates. The project's stricter reading of the pilot used
leave-one-corpus-out selection and grouped bootstrap (`pilot_analysis.md`). Under that reading the originally
planned FORMAL-SI method was not pursued, and the work moved to the H-C analysis and the confirmatory study
of pair provenance. These files are kept unchanged as a record.
